# Strategy handoff — October 20, 2026

Source: a brainstorming chat outside the RetireMeHere project. Add the proposed items to docs/TASKBOARD.md only after review; nothing here has been built.

## Decisions Laurie made

- **No paid reports.** The report offer has had zero sign-ups; remove it.
- **Simplify the site.** Visitors want an answer and leave; data upkeep across all cities is too heavy.
- **"What a week could look like" and neighborhoods** should be easier to reach on their own.
- **Retiring abroad:** interested, but starting with a "try living abroad" guide, not a full scoring engine.
- **Heartwill shelved** (trust and privacy burden too high). Other ideas parked: fitness/rowing content, hotel gym reviews, apartment gym inventories, projects built on the kids' skills.

## Retire abroad

**Parked:** a separate abroad quiz and international city profiles. Revisit only after the US site is simplified, because visa rules change yearly and would add upkeep.
